FM-1 Flash Scan

Reads the FM-1's flash above the app (0x93000–0xFFFFF) and reports, per 4 KB sector, whether it is erased or holds data. Read-only: nothing is written. Needs an OPX build with the FLASH_SCAN command (cmd 120).
