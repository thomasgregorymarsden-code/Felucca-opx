Felucca-OPX: Felucca with an altered-FM engine (OPX) after the Korg opsix, by the Felucca-OPX contributors; not affiliated with Korg or Hügelton Instruments.

    Font: Inter Tight (The Inter Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1). Samples: Versilian Studios VSCO-2 Community Edition and VCSL (CC0 1.0).
